Help & Safety

Support & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about safety, reporting, and recovering your belongings.

🤖 How does the ML Cosine Similarity Search work? +
Our search engine utilizes Machine Learning TF-IDF (Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency) vectorization and Cosine Similarity. When you enter a keyword or partial search term, it computes the vector angle against registered items and ranks the highest-percentage matches with visual match badges.
🔒 Is my personal phone number or contact info exposed? +
No. All communication is held directly inside the platform through the integrated Live Chat and Voice Call tools. You never have to publish private phone numbers or personal emails.
📊 Where can I check how many products I lost or found? +
Check right next to your profile avatar in the header! You can see your live 0 Lost · 0 Found counters. Clicking your profile badge opens your full activity history with options to review or delete resolved reports.
🛡️ Safe handover guidelines? +
Always arrange item handovers in busy, well-lit public campus or workplace areas (reception, library foyer, cafeteria). Ask the owner to verify identifying details before handing the item over.
